Privacy Policy

Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland — e-Moeezland Portal & Official Digital Services

Effective Date: 9 August 2026

The Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland is committed to protecting the privacy, security, and integrity of information entrusted to its official digital services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when a person creates or uses an e-Moeezland Portal Account, why that information is collected, how it may be used, how it is protected, and the circumstances under which it may be disclosed.

This Privacy Policy applies generally to the e-Moeezland Portal and official Moeezland digital services that use or rely upon an e-Moeezland Portal Account, unless a particular service provides an additional or more specific privacy notice.

1. Who This Privacy Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to individuals who access or use official digital services of the Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland, including:

  • Citizens of Moeezland;
  • Residents of Moeezland;
  • Visitors accessing eligible digital services;
  • Persons applying for or using governmental services;
  • Persons creating an e-Moeezland Portal Account; and
  • Authorized representatives acting on behalf of another person.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, "Moeezland," "the Emirate," "the Government," "we," "us," or "our" refers to the Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland and the governmental institutions, departments, authorities, offices, and authorized digital services operating under its administration.

2. What Is an e-Moeezland Portal Account?

An e-Moeezland Portal Account is a centralized digital account intended to allow eligible individuals to access official electronic services provided through Moeezland's digital government infrastructure.

Depending on the user's eligibility and the services available to them, an e-Moeezland Portal Account may allow a person to:

  • Authenticate their identity;
  • Access governmental digital services;
  • Submit electronic applications;
  • View or manage eligible governmental information;
  • Receive electronic communications;
  • Access records associated with their account;
  • Digitally sign eligible documents;
  • Submit government forms;
  • Verify their identity; and
  • Use future digital services that integrate with the e-Moeezland Portal.

The e-Moeezland Portal is intended to provide a unified identity and authentication mechanism across participating Moeezland digital services.

3. Information We Collect

When you register for or use an e-Moeezland Portal Account, Moeezland may collect and maintain information necessary to establish, authenticate, secure, and administer your account.

The information collected may include the following categories.

3.1 Identity Information

This may include:

  • Full legal name;
  • Civil ID or other government-issued identifier;
  • Date of birth;
  • Citizenship or residency-related information;
  • Government registration information;
  • Account status; and
  • Other information required to establish or verify eligibility.

4. Account Information

We may collect information associated with your e-Moeezland Portal Account, including:

  • Email address;
  • Account identifier;
  • User identification identifier;
  • Account registration date and time;
  • Last-login date and time;
  • Account status;
  • Authentication information;
  • Account preferences; and
  • Other information required to maintain the account.

Certain technical identifiers may be generated automatically by the authentication or database infrastructure.

These identifiers are used to associate your account with the appropriate governmental records and services.

5. Face Photograph and Biometric Information

Certain e-Moeezland services may require or permit the submission of a facial photograph.

Where collected, the photograph may be stored as part of the individual's governmental identity or account record.

The purpose of collecting a facial photograph may include:

  • Establishing or maintaining an identity record;
  • Identity verification;
  • Government account verification;
  • Preventing impersonation;
  • Preventing fraudulent account creation;
  • Supporting identification documents;
  • Providing identity-related governmental services;
  • Maintaining governmental records; and
  • Supporting authorized governmental digital services.

Where facial information is processed for the purpose of uniquely identifying an individual, it may constitute biometric information.

Moeezland recognizes that facial and biometric information is particularly sensitive and will seek to apply appropriate safeguards to protect it.

A facial photograph is not collected for advertising purposes.

A facial photograph is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

Moeezland does not sell personal data or biometric information.

6. Information Relating to Digital Signatures

An e-Moeezland Portal Account may be used to digitally sign eligible governmental documents.

When you digitally sign a document through an authorized Moeezland service, the system may retain information necessary to establish and verify the digital signature transaction.

This may include:

  • The identity associated with the signing account;
  • The document or transaction being signed;
  • Date and time of signing;
  • Digital-signature information;
  • Transaction identifiers;
  • Authentication information;
  • Verification information; and
  • Technical records associated with the signing event.

These records may be retained to establish the authenticity and integrity of the electronic transaction and to provide an auditable record of the signing activity.

A digital signature record may therefore form part of an official governmental transaction record.

7. Technical and Security Information

When you access an official Moeezland digital service, technical information may be automatically generated or recorded.

Depending on the service and its technical configuration, this may include:

  • IP address;
  • Browser type;
  • Operating system;
  • Device information;
  • Login timestamps;
  • Session information;
  • Authentication events;
  • Security events;
  • Error logs;
  • Network information;
  • Account activity;
  • Service-access records; and
  • Other technical information necessary for security and operation.

This information may be used to maintain the security, reliability, and functionality of governmental digital services.

8. Why We Collect Your Information

Personal information is collected only for legitimate governmental, administrative, security, identity, or service-related purposes applicable to the relevant digital service.

Purposes may include:

Identity and Authentication

To establish and verify that an account belongs to the person claiming to own it.

Government Service Delivery

To provide electronic governmental services requested by the user.

Account Administration

To create, maintain, authenticate, secure, and manage e-Moeezland Portal Accounts.

Digital Signatures

To allow users to digitally sign eligible governmental documents and maintain records of signing transactions.

Fraud Prevention

To detect and prevent fraudulent registrations, impersonation, unauthorized access, and misuse of governmental services.

Security

To protect government systems, databases, users, and digital infrastructure.

Record Keeping

To maintain accurate governmental and administrative records where required.

Communication

To communicate with users concerning their accounts, applications, transactions, security events, and governmental services.

9. We Do Not Sell Your Personal Data

Moeezland Does Not Sell Personal Data

The Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland does not sell, rent, or commercially trade personal information belonging to citizens, residents, visitors, or other users of its official digital services.

This includes, where applicable:

  • Names;
  • Civil IDs;
  • Dates of birth;
  • Email addresses;
  • Government account information;
  • Face photographs;
  • Biometric information;
  • Digital-signature information;
  • Government service records; and
  • Other personal information collected through official digital services.

Personal information is not provided to advertisers for the purpose of selling advertisements based on the user's governmental identity or personal profile.

Your governmental information is not a product for sale.

10. We Do Not Sell Facial or Biometric Information

Moeezland specifically prohibits the commercial sale of facial photographs or biometric information collected through its official digital services.

Facial and biometric information is intended to be used only for legitimate purposes associated with identity, authentication, security, governmental services, and other authorized governmental functions.

It must not be commercially sold as a dataset.

11. How We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your e-Moeezland Portal Account;
  • Authenticate your identity;
  • Verify your eligibility for governmental services;
  • Provide requested digital services;
  • Process applications;
  • Maintain government records;
  • Enable digital signatures;
  • Prevent fraud and impersonation;
  • Detect unauthorized access;
  • Protect government infrastructure;
  • Investigate security incidents;
  • Communicate with you;
  • Maintain audit records;
  • Improve the reliability and security of digital services; and
  • Perform other purposes directly related to the operation of authorized governmental services.

We will not intentionally use personal information for purposes fundamentally unrelated to the reason for which it was collected without an appropriate basis or notice where required.

12. Access to Personal Information

Access to personal information should be restricted to authorized individuals, systems, departments, or service providers that have a legitimate need to access the information for an authorized governmental or technical purpose.

Not every government employee automatically has access to every citizen's information.

Access may be controlled through:

  • Authentication;
  • Authorization;
  • Role-based permissions;
  • Database security rules;
  • Administrative controls;
  • Audit logging;
  • Technical restrictions; and
  • Other security measures.

13. Governmental Disclosure

Moeezland may disclose or provide access to personal information where necessary for an authorized governmental function, security purpose, administrative process, legal requirement, or provision of a requested service.

Disclosure may occur between authorized governmental institutions or systems where such access is necessary and permitted.

Personal information should not be disclosed merely because an individual requests it.

Access to another person's governmental information requires appropriate authorization.

14. Service Providers and Technical Infrastructure

Official digital services may rely on third-party technology providers for infrastructure, hosting, authentication, databases, communications, security, analytics, storage, or other technical functions.

Where third-party infrastructure is used to process information on behalf of Moeezland, access should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant technical service.

Third-party providers do not automatically acquire ownership of the personal information processed through Moeezland services.

Where applicable, third-party providers may be subject to contractual, technical, security, or confidentiality requirements.

15. Firebase and Cloud Infrastructure

Certain Moeezland digital services may use cloud infrastructure and database technologies to store and process governmental account information.

Where such infrastructure is used, information may be processed within the relevant cloud environment in accordance with the technical architecture and security configuration of the service.

Cloud infrastructure providers may technically process data on behalf of the service as part of providing hosting, database, authentication, storage, security, or related infrastructure.

The use of a cloud infrastructure provider does not mean that Moeezland sells the information stored within that infrastructure.

16. Information Security

Moeezland takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, destruction, or misuse.

Security measures may include:

  • Authentication;
  • Access controls;
  • Database security rules;
  • Encryption where appropriate;
  • Secure communications;
  • Administrative permissions;
  • Security monitoring;
  • Audit logging;
  • Account protection;
  • Fraud detection;
  • Backup and recovery measures; and
  • Other appropriate security controls.

No digital system can be guaranteed to be completely immune from every possible security threat.

Accordingly, users should also protect their passwords, authentication information, devices, and account credentials.

17. Account Security

You are responsible for protecting credentials associated with your e-Moeezland Portal Account.

You should:

  • Use a secure password;
  • Keep authentication information confidential;
  • Avoid sharing account credentials;
  • Log out of shared devices;
  • Keep your email account secure; and
  • Report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

Moeezland personnel should not ordinarily request your password or authentication code.

18. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Maintain an active government account;
  • Provide governmental services;
  • Maintain official records;
  • Verify historical transactions;
  • Maintain digital-signature records;
  • Protect against fraud;
  • Investigate security incidents;
  • Fulfill administrative requirements; or
  • Satisfy applicable legal or governmental record-keeping requirements.

Different categories of information may therefore have different retention periods.

The deletion or deactivation of an e-Moeezland Portal Account does not necessarily mean that all associated governmental records are immediately deleted.

Certain records may need to remain available as official records or for security, administrative, legal, or historical purposes.

19. Account Deactivation

A user may be able to request the deactivation of an e-Moeezland Portal Account where the relevant service permits it.

Deactivation may prevent the user from accessing digital services through that account.

However, deactivation does not necessarily require the deletion of governmental records that must reasonably be retained.

Where applicable, users may be informed about the consequences of account deactivation before the process is completed.

20. Accuracy of Personal Information

Users should ensure that information associated with their account is accurate and current where they are permitted to update it.

Incorrect information may affect:

  • Identity verification;
  • Government applications;
  • Digital signatures;
  • Government records;
  • Eligibility for services; or
  • Account access.

Where an information correction mechanism is available, users should use the designated governmental process.

21. Children's Information

Certain Moeezland services may have specific age or eligibility requirements.

Where a service is intended for or accessible by individuals under the applicable age threshold, additional safeguards may apply to the processing of their information.

The relevant service may establish additional requirements for parental or guardian authorization where appropriate.

22. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Official Moeezland websites may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to:

  • Maintain login sessions;
  • Remember preferences;
  • Provide secure authentication;
  • Protect against abuse;
  • Maintain website functionality;
  • Understand technical errors; or
  • Improve service performance.

Cookies used for authentication and security may be necessary for the operation of certain services.

Moeezland does not use governmental identity information to sell targeted advertising.

23. Analytics

Where analytics or technical monitoring tools are used, they should be used for legitimate purposes such as:

  • Understanding technical performance;
  • Detecting errors;
  • Measuring service reliability;
  • Improving accessibility;
  • Preventing abuse; and
  • Understanding general service usage.

Analytics should not be interpreted as permission to commercially sell users' governmental identity information.

24. Digital Government Records

Information generated through governmental transactions may become part of official records.

Examples may include:

  • Account registration records;
  • Government applications;
  • Digital-signature records;
  • Identity verification records;
  • Administrative transactions;
  • Service requests;
  • Security logs; and
  • Other governmental records.

Such records may be retained according to applicable governmental requirements.

25. Privacy of Other Individuals

Users must not attempt to obtain, access, copy, disclose, or distribute another person's personal information without appropriate authorization.

Governmental databases are not public directories simply because they are maintained electronically.

Unauthorized access to another person's personal information may constitute a violation of the applicable Terms of Use and may result in administrative or other action where permitted.

26. International and Cross-Border Processing

Where technical infrastructure, service providers, or other authorized systems process information outside Moeezland or Pakistan, such processing may be subject to the applicable contractual, technical, governmental, and legal requirements governing the relevant service.

Where cross-border processing occurs, appropriate safeguards should be applied where required.

27. Data Breach and Security Incidents

If Moeezland becomes aware of a significant security incident affecting personal information, it may investigate the incident and take appropriate containment, remediation, and notification measures where required or considered appropriate.

Security incidents may include:

  • Unauthorized access;
  • Data disclosure;
  • Account compromise;
  • Malware;
  • Database compromise;
  • Credential theft; or
  • Other unauthorized activity.

This Privacy Policy should therefore be read together with any applicable service-specific privacy notice.
