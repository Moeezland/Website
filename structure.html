Structure

The Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland is founded on a clear separation of powers between the Legislature, the Executive, the Judiciary, and Public Administration, ensuring accountable and effective governance for all citizens and residents.

Legislature

The Moeezland Parliament (Quorum) is the legislative branch. It is a single-chamber parliament representing the people and is responsible for debating and passing laws. Acts passed by Parliament require Emiri Assent before becoming law.

Executive

The Emir is the Head of State and exercises executive authority together with the Council of Ministers. The Prime Minister leads the Government and coordinates the work of the ministries, while ministers are responsible for administering their respective departments.

Judiciary

The Judiciary is independent and is responsible for interpreting and applying the law. It consists of the Court of Cassation, Courts of Appeal, and Courts of First Instance, with the Court of Cassation serving as the highest court and having final constitutional jurisdiction.

Administration

Public administration is carried out by the national Government and local authorities. Moeezland is divided into Governorates and Municipalities, with Governors appointed by the Emir and Municipalities administered by Emiri Administrators and Local Councils under national law.
