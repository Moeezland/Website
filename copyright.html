All original works, creations, materials, designs, concepts, systems, projects, publications, digital properties, software, written works, visual works, audiovisual works, governmental platforms, websites, portals, documentation, databases, graphics, interfaces, logos, insignia, and other intellectual creations personally created, designed, authored, developed, commissioned, or otherwise lawfully owned by H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I, lawful citizen of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan and Emir of the Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland, shall be regarded as the intellectual property of H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I, subject to applicable law and any expressly stated third-party rights.

H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I reserves all rights in and to such works.

No person, organization, government, institution, company, association, micronation, community, website, platform, server, or other entity may presume that publication, accessibility, visibility, or availability of any such work constitutes a transfer, surrender, abandonment, or waiver of ownership or intellectual property rights.

Publication does not constitute permission.

Accessibility does not constitute permission.

Possession of a copy does not constitute ownership.

Public visibility does not constitute public-domain status.

This declaration applies, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, to original works created or owned by H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I, including but not limited to:

2.1 Websites and Digital Platforms

All websites, webpages, web applications, portals, dashboards, administrative systems, electronic government services, online platforms, digital services, and associated digital infrastructure created by H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I.

  • Internal governmental systems
  • Public-facing governmental services
  • Future websites and digital platforms subsequently created by H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I

The fact that an individual website or portal is operated for the Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland does not, by itself, eliminate or transfer the underlying intellectual property rights of its original creator.

7. Branding and Identity

No person or organization may falsely represent itself as being officially affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or representing the Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland or H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I.

Original database structures, compilations, arrangements, documentation, interfaces, and other protectable elements created by H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I are reserved.

This declaration does not necessarily claim ownership over individual facts or information that cannot legally be protected by copyright.

Where a database contains third-party information, personal information, public records, or material belonging to another party, the rights of those parties remain unaffected.

9. No Implied License

Unless H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I or an expressly authorized representative provides written permission, no license shall be implied merely because a work is:

  • Available online;
  • Accessible without a password;
  • Published on a government website;
  • Displayed publicly;
  • Available for viewing;
  • Available without payment;
  • Accessible through a public portal;
  • Indexed by a search engine;
  • Shared through social media;
  • Distributed electronically; or
  • Otherwise publicly accessible.

Viewing a work does not grant ownership of the work.

10. Prohibited Uses

Except where expressly permitted by law or authorized by the rights holder, no person may reproduce, redistribute, republish, sell, license, sublicense, commercially exploit, modify, adapt, translate, reverse engineer, scrape, mirror, clone, or otherwise exploit protected works belonging to H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I in a manner that infringes applicable rights.

In particular, unauthorized parties may not:

  1. Claim authorship of the works;
  2. Remove copyright notices;
  3. Present the works as their own;
  4. Clone websites or portals;
  5. Copy source code;
  6. Reproduce original designs;
  7. Redistribute proprietary materials;
  8. Sell copies of protected materials;
  9. Create unauthorized derivative works;
  10. Imply governmental authorization;
  11. Use protected branding to create false affiliation;
  12. Repackage protected works as another person's creation; or
  13. Otherwise interfere with the rights reserved by the rights holder.

11. Attribution Does Not Equal Permission

Providing credit, attribution, a link, acknowledgment, or reference to H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I does not automatically constitute permission to reproduce protected material where authorization is otherwise required.

Likewise, failure to provide attribution does not transfer ownership to the person reproducing the work.

Permission and attribution are separate matters.

12. Personal Authorship

H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I retains authorship rights in original works personally created by him, subject to applicable law, contractual arrangements, employment arrangements, commissioned-work arrangements, third-party contributions, and other circumstances that may affect ownership.

Where another person has contributed original material to a work, that person's legally applicable rights remain respected.

Nothing in this declaration is intended to claim ownership over material that H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I does not legally own.

13. Third-Party Materials

Not every component appearing on a Moeezland website necessarily belongs to H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I.

Third-party software, fonts, libraries, frameworks, photographs, icons, APIs, trademarks, open-source components, datasets, and other materials remain subject to their respective owners and licenses.

This declaration applies to the original material owned or controlled by H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I and does not override the lawful rights of third parties.

This declaration shall also apply to future original works created by H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I after the date of publication, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

A new website, portal, software project, document, design, digital service, publication, or other original creation does not require a separate copyright notice merely to establish that the work is intended to remain protected.

Individual projects may nevertheless contain additional copyright notices, licenses, terms of use, or ownership statements where appropriate.

ALL RIGHTS NOT EXPRESSLY GRANTED ARE RESERVED.

No person, organization, institution, company, government, micronation, online community, or other entity should interpret the publication of a work by H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I as a surrender of copyright or intellectual-property rights.

Where applicable, enforcement may include requests for removal, takedown notices, platform complaints, demands to cease unauthorized use, claims for damages or other remedies, and legal proceedings before an appropriate authority.

17. No Transfer Through Publication

The publication of a work on an official website, government portal, social-media account, repository, document archive, public database, or other publicly accessible platform does not, by itself, transfer copyright ownership to the public.

Unless an explicit license or statement provides otherwise, rights remain reserved.

18. Governmental and Personal Rights

The Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland may operate, publish, maintain, or administer websites and digital services containing works created by H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I.

Where appropriate, the Emirate may receive authorization, licensing, or other rights to use such works for governmental purposes.

Such governmental use does not necessarily constitute a transfer of the underlying copyright ownership from the original rights holder.

Any separate agreement governing ownership or licensing shall take precedence over this general declaration to the extent applicable.

19. Governing Principle

The fundamental principle of this declaration is simple:

If an original work was created by H.H. Emir Abdul Moeez I and is legally protectable, the rights associated with that work are reserved unless he has expressly granted permission, transferred the rights, or applicable law provides otherwise.

The existence of the work on the Internet does not make it free to copy.

The existence of the work on a government website does not make it free to copy.

The existence of the work on a public portal does not make it free to copy.

The fact that someone can access it does not mean that someone owns it.

Access is not ownership.

Visibility is not ownership.

A copy is not ownership.

Attribution is not permission.
